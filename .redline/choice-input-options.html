Current issue

Radio and checkbox behavior share one visual grammar

The SDK currently hides both native controls and renders every option as an identical segmented pill. Users only discover whether a question is single-choice or multiple-choice by trying it.

Design target

Communicate selection cardinality before interaction, reinforce it after selection, and keep every target at least 44px high.

Shape-coded segments

Smallest SDK change

Keep the existing compact segmented control, but expose the familiar circle versus square silhouette inside every option.
