The phone stopped throwing away what you type

The companion app reconnected every ~4 seconds and closed the keyboard each time. The daemon now judges drain progress, the phone asks for a bounded replay, and the composer stays editable offline.

~4 s → 0reconnect cycle; 0 per minute on every link tested
34 → 0.6 MBreplay to the phone, once per connection
587 → 0MB per minute wasted over the WiFi proxy

Root cause

Phone connects — backoff resets on open
Daemon replays 34 MB — every chat's full history
Next broadcast trips the 32 MiB cap on bufferedAmount
Close 1013 “Client is not consuming output”
Composer turns read-only — iOS dismisses the keyboard
↺ reconnect after 500 ms, every ~4 s at ~10 MB/s

What changed
