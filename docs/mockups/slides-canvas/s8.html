Takeaways

What to remember

  • ✓Never make an input read-only for a network state. iOS takes the keyboard away.
  • ✓Reset backoff on a healthy connection, not on open.
  • ✓Replay what the screen needs. “Load earlier” fetches the rest.
Residual risk

A client that never reads is still dropped

After about 60 s: 30 s with no drain plus ws's close timeout. That is the intended backstop.
